** Silkcrawler Breeder: Husbandry category (PASTURE_ is the only room type mapped to Husbandry -
** see doc/textiles/06_misc_notes.md). Zero-input, like every vanilla pasture - the game
** engine does not support IN on a pasture INDUSTRY ("Can't declare inputs to a pasture
** industry", confirmed via crash). Mulberry-feeding is abstracted away, same as how Onx's
** grazing is abstracted rather than consuming a tracked grass resource.
** The animal (SILKCRAWLER.txt, formerly SILKWORM.txt) reuses Balticrawler's stats/sprite
** wholesale - MASS:1 was actually the densest-possible pasture population the engine allows,
** denser than any vanilla pasture; the reskin fixes both the visual (wandering reads as normal
** for a creature this size) and the density (Balticrawler's MASS:200 gives ~10x fewer
** animals/tile). Deliberately NOT reusing Balticrawler's cave-dwelling side - shelter comes
** from INDOORS:true below alone, matching real silk sheds, not caves. Room/animal/text files
** and tech UNLOCKS_FACTION/BOOST targets were all renamed SILKWORM->SILKCRAWLER for
** consistency with the reskin. See doc/textiles/06_misc_notes.md.

** BONUS.CLIMATE models real Bombyx mori temperature sensitivity: narrow optimal band
** (~24-28C), poor cold tolerance, and heat stress/disease risk above ~30-35C - historically
** cultivated in temperate China, not tropical or arctic. HOT is a penalty here, not a bonus
** like the old value - excess heat hurts real silk cultivation.
